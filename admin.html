Product sales = order totals by delivery date, excluding failed payments (the same as the Sales Summary), so they include delivery charges collected from customers. Memberships = verified membership payments (monthly, annual and renewals) by the date they were recorded; rows marked Test in the Memberships sheet are ignored. Purchases, Delivery and Refunds come from the Expenses tab, by their date. Profit = Product sales + Memberships − Purchases − Delivery − Refunds. Running total adds each month's profit to the months before it.
