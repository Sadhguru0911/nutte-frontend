FAQ
Questions, answered

Ordering and delivery

Do I have to be a member to order?
No. Anyone can shop. Members pay lower prices; everyone else pays the regular price.
When will my order arrive?
Orders close every Thursday at 9 PM. We pool them with your society's batch and deliver by the following Wednesday. If you order after the cut-off, you're in the next week's batch. The cart and checkout show the exact dates.
What does delivery cost?
₹50 flat within 10 km of Sarjapur Road. Beyond 10 km we charge the actual delivery cost, and we confirm it with you on WhatsApp before dispatch, never after.
How should I store dry fruits?
Keep them in an airtight container, away from sunlight and heat. In humid weather, refrigerating helps them stay fresh longer.

Membership

What does membership cost?
₹400 a month, or ₹3,999 a year (about ₹333 a month). Both renew automatically through AutoPay on Cashfree's secure page.
How much will I save?
About 15% on average against the regular price, and up to 35% on some items. What you save depends on what you buy. The monthly plan pays for itself at roughly ₹2,700 of regular-price shopping a month, and the annual plan at roughly ₹2,200 a month.
How do I cancel?
You can cancel anytime; it stops future auto-renewal charges. If you paid by UPI, open your UPI app, go to AutoPay / Mandates and cancel "CommunitE". If you paid by card, tap ⭐ Member at the top of the page, then Cancel auto-renewal, and we'll cancel the card mandate within 24 hours. Please cancel at least 2 days before your renewal date.
Do I get a refund if I cancel?
Monthly plan: no refund for the current month; your membership stays active until the end of the month you've paid for.
Annual plan: your membership stays active until the end of your current paid month, counted from the day you joined (a part month counts as a full month). We refund the rest: ₹3,999 minus ₹400 for each month used, back to your original payment method within 7 working days.
If a renewal is charged by mistake or twice, we refund it in full.

Returns and refunds

Can I return something?
Because we sell food, we don't accept returns for change of mind or personal taste. If a product arrives damaged, is the wrong item, or has a genuine quality issue, we'll replace it.
What if something is wrong with my order?
Message us on WhatsApp or call +91 89510 48013 within 10 days of delivery, with a photo of the product. Once we confirm the issue, we arrange a replacement with your next delivery cycle.
What if I was charged twice?
Message us on WhatsApp with the payment details and we'll refund the extra charge to your original payment method. See our Refund Policy.

🎲Tambola

Each ticket follows standard Tambola rules — 15 numbers per ticket, 5 per row, arranged by column.

—

0 of 90 numbers called

How to play

Tambola (Housie) is meant to be played with everyone in the same room. One person hosts — they open the Number Caller tab and call out numbers one at a time. Everyone else plays from their own ticket (printed, or the tappable link) and crosses off matching numbers as they're called.

Claiming a win

The moment a player completes a pattern, they call it out loud immediately — e.g. "Top Line!" or "Housie!" The host pauses calling, checks that player's ticket against the numbers already called, and confirms or rejects the claim out loud before continuing. This keeps everything fair, transparent, and settled in the room in real time.

Common winning patterns

  • 🐇
    Early Five
    first player to cross any 5 numbers, in any order
  • ⬆️
    Top Line
    all 5 numbers in the top row
  • ➖
    Middle Line
    all 5 numbers in the middle row
  • ⬇️
    Bottom Line
    all 5 numbers in the bottom row
  • 📐
    Four Corners
    the first and last numbers of the top row, plus the first and last numbers of the bottom row
  • 🏠
    Full House (Housie)
    all 15 numbers on the ticket

The host can also announce their own house rules or extra patterns (like a "Star" or "Second Full House") before starting — just make sure everyone agrees on the patterns and prizes before the first number is called.

A note on prizes

This is a free tool for casual, in-person community fun — CommunitE doesn't run, organize, or take a share of any prize pool, and doesn't facilitate real-money wagering in any way. If you and your group choose to play for prizes, that's entirely between the players — we'd suggest keeping it to non-cash prizes (discount codes, small gifts, bragging rights) to stay clearly on the safe, informal side. You're responsible for following whatever local rules apply to your group if real money is ever involved.
